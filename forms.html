Patient forms

Get your paperwork done before your first visit

Every new primary care patient completes a packet before we schedule an appointment. Fill it out online, or print it and drop it off or fax it to us.

Call first to confirm we're taking new patients. We may stop accepting new primary care patients at any time. Call 409.210.3336, or type "new patient" in the Facebook chat on our homepage.

How it works

  1. STEP 1

    Call to confirm

    Make sure we're accepting new patients before you start your packet.

  2. STEP 2

    Complete your packet

    Use the online form or the printable PDF. Adults 18+ and children have separate packets.

  3. STEP 3

    Send it with your insurance card

    Include a copy of the front and back of your card. Drop it off at the clinic or fax it.

  4. STEP 4

    We'll call you

    Processing takes 4–7 business days. Once it's done, we call to schedule your first appointment.
