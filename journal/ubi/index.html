The Cure That Time Forgot: Ultraviolet Blood Irradiation

The rise of antibiotics overshadowed this “miracle treatment.”

By gerstenberg.clinicDecember 20214 min read

In 1904, Dr. Niels Finsen was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for using ultraviolet (UV) light therapy to cure 98% of various skin conditions, mostly a skin form of tuberculosis.

A few years later during the Spanish Flu pandemic of 1918, Dr. Emmett K. Knott pursued innovating UV light therapy. He built on Finsen’s work with the concept of exposing blood to UV as a method of helping people to conquer the aggressive virus. His hypothesis was that the key to Finsen’s success was due to the blood in the small capillaries of the irradiated skin. Irradiation is the action of exposing something (in this case - blood) to radiation (in this case, ultraviolet radiation).

His experiments showed drastic improvement in the reduction of bacteria and recovery from infection, and he found the optimum treatment was only 5–7% of the estimated blood volume. The first ultraviolet blood irradiation treatment for a human wasn’t done till 1928, as a last resort for a dying woman. She made a full recovery, going on to bear two children.1

In the 40s and 50s, many diseases were treated by ultraviolet blood irradiation (UBI), including septicemia, pneumonia, tuberculosis, arthritis, asthma and even poliomyelitis. Although physicians in the United States were the first to study this treatment and published results in the American Journal of Surgery, with the rise of antibiotics, UBI treatment was largely displaced. Now it’s been called, “the cure that time forgot.”2

Later studies were primarily done in Russia and eastern countries, while the west has mostly dismissed UBI. Modern clinics such as the well-known Riordan Clinic in Kansas use UBI to pursue these results:3

  • Heightens the body’s immune response
  • Powerful anti-inflammatory and anti-infection effects
  • Improves circulation
  • Oxygenation of tissues
  • Balancing effect (homeostasis)
  • Reduction of tissue pain
  • Increased immune and pain tolerance to radiation or chemotherapy
  • Cardiovascular protection through increased metabolism of cholesterol, uric acid, and glucose
  • Stimulation for production of red blood cells
  • Improvement in the flow and properties of the blood (rheology: see below list)

Other effects listed by another prominent US clinic4 remarks UBI aids in:

The reduction of:

  • Cholesterol
  • Creatinine levels
  • Blood lactate
  • Clot formation tendencies
  • Blood viscosity
  • Uric acid levels
  • Fibrin levels
  • Plasma viscosity
  • Surface tension of the blood

Normalization or modulation of:

  • Immune status
  • Glucose tolerance
  • Fibrinolysis

With drug resistant bacterial strains and their related infections becoming a more prevalent problem, as well as auto-immune disease and other disease caused by undue inflammation, UBI ought to be brought back into the mainstream as a powerful treatment option.

Who may not be treated by UBI?

Patients with the following conditions should very strongly consider other treatment for their conditions, UBI is not recommended with:

  • Pregnancy
  • Porphyria
  • G6PD
  • Hereditary hemophilia
  • Recent heart attack (within 3 months)

What to do just after my UBI treatments?

  • Drink plenty of water during the next 24 hours, at least 6-8 glasses of purified water.
  • Eat a non-GMO diet rich in green, leafy vegetables, or you may use a ‘superfood’ smoothie for recovery.
  • Ensure you are clearing your bowels properly.
  • Take enzymes and minerals to aid the energy production process inside each cell of your body.
  • Avoid taking antioxidant supplements for 24 hours afterwards, such as vitamins C, A, E, and glutathione.
  • Light activity only if you experience any fatigue or “draw down.” A transient reaction of mild flu-like symptoms occurs in 10-20% of new UBI patients. This is part of the body’s detoxifying process.
  • Do all the above recommendations and consider a sauna or hot tub (less than 99 degrees).
  • Afterward, patients describe feeling the best they have in a long time!
