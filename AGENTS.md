# Guidance for agents

## Purpose of this website

Gerstenberg Clinic is a holistic family practice led by a Doctor of Osteopathic
Medicine (DO) in Southeast Texas, offering primary care and alternative medicine
services. This website is a sales and marketing site at the top of the clinic's
patient acquisition funnel. Its central purpose is to introduce prospective
patients to the clinic and help them discover services, especially new offerings
they may not know are available.

Use that purpose to guide content, design, navigation, and implementation
decisions throughout this repository.

## Help visitors discover services and take the next step

- Write for prospective patients who may be unfamiliar with the clinic or a
  treatment. Use welcoming, plain language and explain unfamiliar terms.
- Connect each service to the patient concerns it addresses, explain what it
  involves, and make its potential benefits easy to understand.
- Give new and relevant services clear paths from the homepage, navigation, and
  related content so visitors can discover and explore them.
- Guide visitors from awareness to learning more, then to an appropriate next
  step such as contacting the clinic or requesting a consultation. Use clear,
  specific calls to action and verified contact or scheduling destinations.
- Preserve access to existing patient tools while keeping service discovery and
  prospective patient inquiries central to the public site's experience.
- Favor readable, accessible, mobile-friendly pages and straightforward journeys
  that build trust and reduce friction.

## Build trust with accurate information

- Keep marketing persuasive, respectful, and grounded in verified clinic
  offerings. Do not invent services, credentials, testimonials, pricing,
  availability, or clinical evidence.
- Describe potential benefits accurately without promising cures, guaranteed
  outcomes, or universal suitability. Flag unsupported medical claims for clinic
  review instead of amplifying them.
- Use educational content to help visitors make informed decisions and encourage
  a conversation with the clinic about their individual needs.

## Working in this repository

This is a Jekyll site. Read `readme.md` for local setup. Preserve existing Liquid
templates, front matter, permalinks, and shared layouts when changing pages.
For site changes, check relevant links, calls to action, and mobile presentation;
run the Jekyll build when the local environment supports it. In your handoff,
state what you checked and any validation limitations.
