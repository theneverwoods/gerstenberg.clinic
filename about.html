Providing the care you need in two different ways

Primary care for the whole family through gerstenberg.clinic, and cash-pay wellness services through gerstenberg.clinic WELLNESS.

Primary care

gerstenberg.clinic

gerstenberg.clinic offers full service primary care, but we are not currently accepting pediatric patients under the age of 4 yrs. Our primary care includes child checkups as well as school physicals, well-women exams and annual wellness visits for Medicare enrollees.

Cash-pay wellness

gerstenberg.clinic WELLNESS

We provide cutting-edge products and services through gerstenberg.clinic WELLNESS, including: vitamins, homeopathic remedies, human-identical hormone therapy, medical weight management options, male ED treatments and more. Look around our clinic and website for materials that detail this line as we are consistently seeking out diversification to offer the very best NON-drug treatments for our patients and community.

Services offered through gerstenberg.clinic WELLNESS are considered cash-pay services and claims are not filed with insurance carriers since these services are not covered by insurance. Some flexible spending accounts (FSA) or health savings accounts (HSA) may reimburse for them and we gladly accept cash, check, credit card, Cherry payment plans and CareCredit for these services.

Our approach

We believe in treating the person as a whole. Health is impacted by many different factors, including; vitamin and nutrient deficiency, hormone imbalance, diet choices, physical exercise, and spiritual/emotional factors.

Because of this, gerstenberg.clinic seeks out new and innovative solutions that target healing exactly where the patient needs it – and avoids the side effects of unnecessary treatment.

We join Hippocrates in saying, “the greatest medicine of all is teaching people not to need it.”

Dr. Gerstenberg received his premedical degree from Lamar University in his hometown Beaumont, TX. He then went on to the University of North Texas Health Sciences Center at Fort Worth to receive his Doctor of Osteopathy degree in 1993. He served his residency at St. Anthony Hospital in Oklahoma City, then went to serve three years in the United States Air Force at Barksdale AFB, Louisiana. After completing his tour of duty, he stayed in Shreveport, Louisiana for two years in private practice. In 2001, he had the opportunity to relocate his wife and four children to Nederland, Texas and build his own practice. Today he has seven children and many wonderful grandchildren.

The clinic Dr. Gerstenberg envisioned practices the full realm of family practice. He has a great interest in preventative medicine, which starts with traditional medical care but has continued to expand into alternative and complementary options as his patients demanded them, now through gerstenberg.clinic WELLNESS. He focuses an increasing amount of time on addressing the root causes of chronic fatigue issues and on cognitive improvement, and he has many special options for those suffering with treatment-resistant neuropathy.

Dr. Gerstenberg serves as the medical director for the Hope Women’s Resource Center and as health authority for the City of Port Neches. Since 1998 he has also been a Clinical Assistant Professor at the University of North Texas Health Sciences Center at Fort Worth-Texas College of Osteopathic Medicine. He does the same with students from the Sam Houston State University College of Osteopathic Medicine and serves on the Lamar University School of Nursing Advisory Board. The clinic works with other local educational centers focusing on healthcare clinical and preclinical education.
