Why stacking works

Five hours of recovery work, done in one half hour

Most recovery tools work one pathway at a time. A sauna heats you. A PEMF mat pulses you. An oxygen bar gives you oxygen. Getting all of them means five appointments, five drives and most of a day.

A stacked session runs them together, and they amplify each other. Heat opens the skin so it takes in ozone and carbonic acid. Carbonic acid helps your blood release oxygen into tissue while you breathe concentrated oxygen. That is why the response compounds instead of adding up one piece at a time.

How it stacks

Each technology primes the next

Everything starts at once, but the effects build on each other in this order.

  1. Heat opens the door

    Far infrared and steam raise your core temperature about 3°F and open your pores. Heat stress also prompts cells to make heat shock proteins, which help other proteins hold their shape.

  2. Skin takes in the gases

    With pores open, ozone and carbonic acid carried in the steam are absorbed through the skin instead of only the lungs.

  3. Blood lets go of oxygen

    Higher carbon dioxide in tissue tells hemoglobin to release more of its oxygen (the Bohr effect), while you breathe concentrated oxygen. Oxygen delivery to tissue rises 40 to 50 percent.

  4. Cells put it to work

    Pulsed magnetic fields and microcurrent work alongside your cells' own electrical signals, supporting mitochondria as they turn that oxygen into ATP, the energy every cell runs on.

The ten technologies

What each one does in your body

Heat

Raises core temperature and opens the skin

Far infrared heat

Warms tissue from the inside rather than only heating the air. Whole-body heat raises core temperature, increases circulation and prompts cells to make heat shock proteins.

Steam

Moist heat raises skin temperature quickly, starts sweating sooner and carries ozone, carbonic acid and essential oils to the skin.

Gases & breath

Changes how much oxygen reaches your tissues

Transdermal ozone

A highly active form of oxygen generated from pure oxygen and absorbed through open pores. Research on low-dose ozone suggests it acts as a mild signal that prompts the body to upregulate its own antioxidant pathways.

Transdermal carbonic acid

Carbon dioxide absorbed through the skin widens small blood vessels and, through the Bohr effect, helps red blood cells release oxygen where tissue needs it.

Concentrated oxygen breathing

You breathe concentrated oxygen the whole session while heat raises your heart rate much like light exercise, so more oxygen loads into circulation.

Essential oil infusion

Oils added to the steam reach you through the skin and the air you breathe, and many clients find they make the heat easier to breathe through.

Fields & light

Works with your cells' own electrical and light signals

High-intensity PEMF

Pulsed electromagnetic fields pass through the whole body. Studies associate PEMF with cell membrane activity, mitochondrial ATP production and improved local circulation.

Frequency-specific microcurrent

Very low electrical currents, close in scale to the body's own signals. Research suggests microcurrent can support ATP production in the tissue it reaches.

Photon light & color

Specific wavelengths of visible light during the session. Research on light exposure links wavelength to alertness and mood.

Ultraviolet irradiation

Natural byproduct. As ozone breaks down during the session, it naturally produces UV energy — a byproduct that supports the body’s natural processes.
