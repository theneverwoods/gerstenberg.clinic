You shouldn’t have to worry about how to get the best medical care. That’s why we’re pleased to accept the CareCredit healthcare credit card for our wellness services. CareCredit lets you say “yes” to recommended treatment and pay for it in convenient monthly payments that fit your budget.

All services financed with CareCredit must be rendered within 30 days of transaction date, but may be paid for over a 6-month period with deferred interest if the purchase is over $200.
